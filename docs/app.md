# Minimal Phone

This app strips your phone of its addictive and unproductive aspects. It distills the device down into essential functions that is important to everyday life. Such as internet banking, e-wallets, 2FA authenticators, mandatory university apps, etc. 

This app exists because how toxic the phone is sometimes to our well being. Yet, dumbphones can be expensive and downright too restrictive which makes it unpractical for everyday use.

# Mechanism

Minimal Phone limits the apps that can be installed in your phone. It also deletes the browser, since most social media apps have a web version. In the future a limited browser may be included in the package to provide essential support (e.g. for web based OAuth authentication), but because of technical resources reason, it's not yet provided.

Because each country usually has its own ecosystem of apps (e.g. different banks, e-wallets, universities), the list of allowed apps is governed by the community. For edge cases, you may enable the experimental LLM-as-a-judge feature (coming soon) (more important for the novel limited web browser feature).
